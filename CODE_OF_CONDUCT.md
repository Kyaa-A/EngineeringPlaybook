# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Sexualized language, imagery, attention, or advances
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within community spaces and when an individual officially represents the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior to **asnaripacalna@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy and security of reporters will be respected.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will use the following Community Impact Guidelines:

### 1. Correction

For inappropriate or unprofessional behavior: a private written warning and clarification. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning

For a violation through one incident or a series of actions: a warning with consequences for continued behavior and a defined period of no interaction with affected people or moderators.

### 3. Temporary Ban

For a serious violation: a temporary ban from community interaction or public communication.

### 4. Permanent Ban

For a sustained pattern of violations, harassment, aggression, or disparagement: permanent removal from community interaction.
